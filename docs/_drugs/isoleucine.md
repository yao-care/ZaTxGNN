## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available for isoleucine as a therapeutic agent. Isoleucine is one of three branched-chain amino acids (with leucine and valine), primarily studied for roles in protein synthesis and muscle/energy metabolism. It has no established pharmacological indication and no marketing license in Taiwan, so there is no "original indication" from which to draw a mechanistic bridge to gastroparesis.

The TxGNN score of 99.32% reflects similarity in the model's learned knowledge-graph embedding space, not a validated biological or clinical link. There is no published evidence — preclinical, observational, or clinical — establishing a causal pathway between BCAA supplementation and gastric motility or gastric emptying. Without a mechanistic anchor (original indication, known MOA, or supporting trials/literature), this prediction should be treated as a hypothesis-generating signal only, not as evidence of therapeutic plausibility.

Given the absence of any corroborating clinical or mechanistic data, this candidate currently rests entirely on computational prediction and requires substantial additional evidence before any further evaluation is warranted.
