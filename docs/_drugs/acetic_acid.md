## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available for this drug. Acetic acid is generally known as a topical acidifying and antibacterial agent, which acts by lowering local pH and disrupting microbial growth.

"Post-bacterial disorder" is a vague category. It is not clear which condition it covers, and no specific mechanistic link between acetic acid and sequelae that follow a bacterial infection can be supported from the data compiled for this report. A topical antibacterial effect would act on the bacteria during active infection, not on the later sequelae. The prediction should therefore be treated as a hypothesis generated by the model, not as an established rationale.
