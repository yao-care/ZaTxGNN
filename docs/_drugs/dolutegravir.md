## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known drug class, dolutegravir inhibits retroviral integrase, the enzyme that inserts viral DNA into the host genome. Its efficacy in HIV infection is established.

Feline immunodeficiency virus (FIV) is a lentivirus related to HIV and causes progressive immune dysfunction in cats. A shared integrase target is therefore plausible. However, FIV integrase differs from HIV-1 integrase, and the only supporting publication is a preclinical study of a combination regimen (dolutegravir, tenofovir, emtricitabine) in FIV-infected cats. It does not isolate dolutegravir's contribution.

The high TxGNN score is a computational prediction, not clinical evidence. Because FIV affects cats, this is not a human therapeutic repurposing opportunity.

**Other predictions in the data:**
- **Simian immunodeficiency virus infection** (score 0.9985, L4): supported by nonhuman primate and in vitro studies, including dolutegravir monotherapy in SIV-infected macaques with resistance selection. Its main value is as a research model for HIV, which overlaps with the existing indication.
- **Neurodevelopmental disorder with ataxic gait, absent speech, and decreased cortical white matter** (score 0.9980, L5): no mechanistic link, trials or literature. This likely reflects a network artifact and should be treated as hypothesis-generating only.

## Safety Considerations

**Drug Interactions:** 62 interactions were identified. Major interactions shown in the data are with polyvalent cation products, which can reduce dolutegravir absorption through chelation:
- Antacids and mineral products: aluminium hydroxide, magaldrate, magnesium carbonate/chloride/citrate
- Calcium products: calcium acetate, carbonate, citrate, glubionate, gluconate, lactate, phosphate
- Iron
- Adsorbents: kaolin, attapulgite

Minor interactions include aprepitant, cimetidine, clarithromycin, clotrimazole and dexamethasone.

Please refer to the SAHPRA-approved Professional Information (PI) for warnings, contraindications and dosing separation guidance. Report adverse drug reactions to SAHPRA.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The top predicted indication is a veterinary disease supported by a single preclinical study of a combination regimen, with no clinical trials. It is not a human therapeutic opportunity, and the SIV prediction mainly serves as a research model that overlaps with dolutegravir's existing approved use.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- SAHPRA package insert warnings and contraindications (a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Approved indication text for the registered products
- If a veterinary application is considered, dolutegravir-specific efficacy and safety data in target species, and a veterinary regulatory pathway (outside the scope of SAHPRA human medicine registration)
