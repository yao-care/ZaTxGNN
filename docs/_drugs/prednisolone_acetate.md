## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available for this drug. Prednisolone acetate is a topical corticosteroid, and corticosteroids of this type generally suppress inflammation on the ocular surface. That gives a plausible, general link to inflammatory conjunctivitis.

The record does not list the drug's original approved indications, so the relationship between the original and predicted indications cannot be checked. No trial or paper evaluates prednisolone acetate in this condition. The high score therefore should not be read as clinical support.

Among the other candidates in this prediction set, **vernal conjunctivitis** (rank 9, score 99.58%) has the most supporting material. It has one disease-matched trial and a larger body of literature, including an experimental allergic conjunctivitis study comparing prednisolone acetate 1% with cyclosporine and epinastine. That evidence is still indirect and is classed L4. It is worth considering first if this drug moves forward.
