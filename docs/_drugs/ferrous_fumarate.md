## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available for ferrous fumarate. It is an iron salt used to replenish iron stores and correct iron deficiency.

The data compiled for this report do not support a mechanistic link between oral iron and the predicted condition. Esophageal malformations, such as esophageal atresia or tracheoesophageal fistula, are congenital structural defects that arise during embryonic development. There is no established biological rationale for oral iron supplementation to correct or treat them. The high score (0.9949) is a model-derived output only. The association may reflect shared gene or pathway neighbours in the model's training data and has not been validated. Route compatibility and similarity to the original use have not yet been assessed.
