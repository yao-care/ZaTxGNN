## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general benzodiazepine pharmacology, alprazolam enhances GABA-A receptor signalling, which produces sedative and anxiolytic effects. This gives a plausible basis for sleep-promoting activity.

Insomnia also commonly occurs together with anxiety and panic, so symptom overlap is likely. This is a reasoning link only, not demonstrated efficacy.

None of the retrieved papers is an alprazolam-specific efficacy trial in insomnia. They cover panic disorder, benzodiazepine pharmacokinetics, prescribing trends, economic burden, zolpidem deprescribing and COVID-19 insomnia epidemiology. Because no cited study directly tests alprazolam for insomnia, the evidence level is L5 (model prediction with only indirect literature).

Long-term hypnotic use also carries risks of dependence, tolerance, falls and cognitive impairment, especially in elderly patients.

## Conclusion and Next Steps
