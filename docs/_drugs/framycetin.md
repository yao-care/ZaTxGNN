## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available for this drug. Based on known information, framycetin is an aminoglycoside antibiotic that is poorly absorbed, so it acts mainly where it is applied, such as on the skin, in the ear or in the gut.

A theoretical link exists through the gut-liver axis. Changing the bacteria in the intestine may influence bile duct inflammation, and oral non-absorbable antibiotics such as vancomycin have been explored in primary sclerosing cholangitis. Aminoglycosides, however, are not an established approach in this disease. The high score is a computational prediction with no supporting studies. Systemic toxicity (kidney damage and hearing damage) also limits any use beyond topical or oral application.
