## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data were not available in the data compiled for this report. Published reviews describe tocilizumab as a humanised monoclonal antibody that blocks IL-6 receptor signalling. Its efficacy in rheumatoid arthritis and juvenile idiopathic arthritis is established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to ankylosing spondylitis.

IL-6 is one of several cytokines implicated in the inflammation and bone effects of axial spondyloarthritis, alongside TNF-α. Blocking the IL-6 receptor is therefore biologically plausible. Both diseases are chronic inflammatory rheumatic conditions, and the literature has discussed IL-6 blockade as an option for patients who do not respond to TNF inhibitors.

However, plausibility is not proof. Two large placebo-controlled Phase 3 trials in ankylosing spondylitis were terminated, and their outcomes are not included in the data compiled for this report. The remaining supporting literature consists mainly of reviews and case reports. Tocilizumab is not an established therapy for this condition.

## Safety Considerations

**Drug Interactions:** The interaction screen returned 100 potential interactions, of which 20 were reviewed here. The most notable are:
- **Major:** deferiprone
- **Moderate:** anticoagulants (warfarin, apixaban, rivaroxaban, dicoumarol), statins (simvastatin, rosuvastatin), antimalarials (chloroquine, hydroxychloroquine), nitroimidazoles (metronidazole, tinidazole, secnidazole, benznidazole), naltrexone, disulfiram and lidocaine
- **Minor:** zinc salts (sulfate, acetate, gluconate, chloride)

**Infection risk (from the literature):** Reviews highlight an increased infection risk with biologics, including tuberculosis. Screening and preventive therapy are recommended before treatment, which is particularly relevant in South Africa's high-TB-burden setting.

Please refer to the SAHPRA-approved Professional Information (PI) for full warnings and contraindications. Report adverse drug reactions to SAHPRA.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The biological rationale is plausible, but the two Phase 3 RCTs in ankylosing spondylitis were terminated and no efficacy outcomes are available in the data compiled for this report. The supporting literature is limited to reviews and a single case report. Tocilizumab is not an established AS therapy, so the evidence does not yet justify moving forward.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Published efficacy and safety outcomes from NCT01209689 and NCT01209702 (including the BUILDER-1/2 results and the reason for termination)
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- SAHPRA Professional Information (warnings, contraindications and approved indications) for Actemra 48/30.1/0370
- A TB screening and infection monitoring plan appropriate for South African patients
- Review of other tocilizumab-containing registrations, if any, and EML status
