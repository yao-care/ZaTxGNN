## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Fexofenadine is known as a selective peripheral H1-receptor antagonist. A plausible link is attenuation of histamine-mediated inflammation of the ocular surface.

However, rosacea conjunctivitis is driven mainly by meibomian gland dysfunction, microbial or Demodex factors, and innate immune dysregulation. H1 blockade is therefore likely at best a minor adjunct, not a core treatment. The high TxGNN score (0.9985) probably reflects the model's proximity between this drug and related conjunctival conditions rather than independent evidence for this disease. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis only.
