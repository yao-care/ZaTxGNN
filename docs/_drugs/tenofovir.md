## Tenofovir: From Chronic Hepatitis B and HIV Infection to Simian Immunodeficiency Virus Infection
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Tenofovir is a nucleotide reverse transcriptase inhibitor, recorded in the knowledge graph for chronic hepatitis B virus infection, HIV infection and AIDS.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **simian immunodeficiency virus (SIV) infection**, but this is an animal model of HIV rather than a distinct human disease.
There are **no registered clinical trials** and **20 publications** (mostly non-human primate studies and reviews) behind this prediction.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, tenofovir is a nucleotide reverse transcriptase inhibitor active against HIV-1 and HIV-2. SIV is a closely related retrovirus that also depends on reverse transcriptase, so activity against it is mechanistically expected.

Macaque studies show that tenofovir-based regimens (tenofovir disoproxil fumarate or tenofovir alafenamide, with or without emtricitabine) protect against vaginal and rectal SHIV challenge. Tenofovir monotherapy has also suppressed SIV replication in infected macaques.

However, SIV infection is an animal model used to develop HIV treatments and prevention, not a separate human disease. These data are best read as preclinical support for the existing HIV use, not as a new human indication.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
SIV infection is a non-human primate model of HIV, so the macaque data support the existing HIV use of tenofovir and do not point to a new human indication. There are no clinical trials for this prediction. The evidence level is L4 because several preclinical studies directly address SIV/SHIV infection itself.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Clarification of whether the intended question is a new human indication or support for existing HIV and hepatitis B uses (most likely the latter, in which case no new indication would be needed)
- The SAHPRA Professional Information for warnings, contraindications and approved indications (currently missing)
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Review of the knowledge-graph contraindication relations (especially renal, hepatic and bone-related) against the label

The other two predictions do not support repurposing. Feline acquired immunodeficiency syndrome has only two veterinary studies and no human data (L4, Hold). The rare neurodevelopmental disorder has a high model score but no trials, no literature and no plausible mechanism (L5, Hold).
