## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Praziquantel is known to act on calcium channels and the outer surface (tegument) of schistosomes. Its efficacy in helminth infections is established.

There is no evident link between this action and sarcoma biology. Five of the top 10 predictions are sarcomas: leiomyosarcoma at various sites, retroperitoneal sarcoma, small intestinal sarcoma and dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans. This cluster most likely reflects similarity in the knowledge-graph neighbourhood rather than pharmacology. The high score therefore should not be read as a signal of efficacy.
