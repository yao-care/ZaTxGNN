## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, DL-threonine is an essential amino acid (a racemic mixture) used as a dietary and nutritional supplement and as a component of multi-ingredient parenteral nutrition products.

A plausible but unvalidated link is threonine's role in mucin synthesis and gastrointestinal mucosal integrity, or its role as a precursor of glycine and serine. Neither pathway is established as relevant to gastric motility or to the pathophysiology of gastroparesis, which involves impaired gastric emptying from vagal dysfunction, loss of interstitial cells of Cajal, or enteric neuropathy.

The high score most likely reflects shared amino acid metabolism or nutrition-related neighbours in the model's data rather than a true mechanistic signal. It should be treated as a hypothesis-generating output only. The racemic DL form adds further uncertainty, because only L-threonine is used in protein synthesis and the activity of D-threonine is unclear.
