L-Lysine is an essential amino acid. In South Africa it appears in registered parenteral nutrition products, but the SAHPRA records provided state no approved indication.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available for L-lysine. It is an essential amino acid used nutritionally, including in parenteral nutrition products. No approved indication is recorded in the material provided.

No established link between L-lysine and gastric motility could be identified. The high score (99.77%) is a graph-based prediction only and does not reflect biological or clinical evidence. Gastroparesis involves loss of interstitial cells of Cajal and enteric neurons, leading to delayed gastric emptying. Nothing in the available data connects lysine to that process.

## Clinical Trial Evidence
