## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Adapalene is known as a topical retinoid that acts on retinoic acid receptors (RAR-beta and RAR-gamma), with minimal systemic exposure after skin application.

A raised plasma zinc level is a biochemical finding. No plausible link between retinoid receptor activity on the skin and plasma zinc has been identified. The high TxGNN score reflects a pattern found by the model and is not backed by any trial or published study. The prediction should therefore be treated as a hypothesis only, not as a reasonable therapeutic direction.
