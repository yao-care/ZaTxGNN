## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available for dihydrocodeine. It is an opioid, and opioids act mainly on central cough and pain pathways. They do not act on nasal tissue pathology.

The high score probably reflects similarity to the nasal and upper-airway conditions that dihydrocodeine-containing products are known to be used for symptomatically. It probably does not reflect a new disease-modifying mechanism. Because the mechanism data is missing, the link between the drug and nasal cavity disease cannot be verified. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis only.
