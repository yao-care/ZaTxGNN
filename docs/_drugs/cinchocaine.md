## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Cinchocaine is a local anaesthetic that blocks voltage-gated sodium channels in nerve membranes. Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report, so this description is based on general pharmacological knowledge of the drug class.

A theoretical link to bronchitis would be suppression of airway sensory nerve activity, which drives the cough reflex. Other local anaesthetics, such as lidocaine, have been studied for cough. However, cinchocaine is not established for inhaled or systemic use, and its toxicity profile argues for caution. The high TxGNN score is a computational output only and is not evidence of clinical benefit.
