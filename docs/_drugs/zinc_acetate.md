## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available. Zinc is a cofactor of copper/zinc superoxide dismutase (SOD) and is abundant in the retina. It could therefore modulate oxidative stress in diabetic retinal injury.

Diabetic retinopathy involves oxidative and inflammatory damage to retinal vessels, so an antioxidant role is biologically plausible. This link is unverified. The provided data contains no trial or publication that tests it, and the high score reflects knowledge-graph patterns rather than clinical proof.

The same reasoning applies to the broader entry "diabetic retinopathy" (rank 3, score 99.92%), which also has no clinical or literature evidence.
