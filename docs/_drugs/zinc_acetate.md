## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

No original indication is recorded for this drug in the evidence pack, so a direct comparison between the original and predicted indications cannot be made here.

This is consistent with the L5 evidence tier — a high embedding-similarity score with no corroborating clinical or literature support.
