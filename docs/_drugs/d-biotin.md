## One-Sentence Summary

D-Biotin is a B-complex vitamin. It is a cofactor for enzymes involved in carbohydrate, fat and amino acid metabolism. The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **dyspepsia**. Currently there are **no registered clinical trials** and **6 retrieved publications**, none of which tests biotin as a treatment for dyspepsia.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. What is generally known is that biotin is a cofactor for carboxylase enzymes involved in carbohydrate, fat and amino acid metabolism. Biotin deficiency can cause gastrointestinal symptoms such as nausea and anorexia.

This gives only a loose, non-specific link to functional gut complaints. No retrieved evidence shows that biotin supplementation improves dyspepsia in people who are not deficient. The high TxGNN score (0.9943) is a model output only and does not replace clinical evidence.
