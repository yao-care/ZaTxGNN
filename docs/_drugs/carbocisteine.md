## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Carbocisteine is a mucoregulator with antioxidant activity, but nothing in the data compiled for this report links it to urate metabolism or to the inflammation seen in gout.

The prediction therefore has no identifiable mechanistic support. The high score (0.9967) is not backed by trials or literature, and it is most likely a modelling artefact rather than a genuine pharmacological signal. It should be treated as a hypothesis only.
