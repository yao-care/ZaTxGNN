## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Aripiprazole is a partial agonist at dopamine D2/D3 and serotonin 5-HT1A receptors and an antagonist at 5-HT2A receptors. This profile is consistent with mood stabilisation in bipolar disorder and with boosting the effect of antidepressants in major depression. The DrugBank mechanism-of-action field is not populated for this record, so the description above comes from the pack's mechanistic rationale.

Antipsychotic use in mood disorders is well recognised. A 2016 review notes that aripiprazole, quetiapine and olanzapine have received FDA approval as add-ons to antidepressants in major depressive disorder (PMID 25963405). Several later reviews position aripiprazole as one of the most widely studied augmentation agents in treatment-resistant depression (PMID 37149344).

The prediction is therefore reasonable, but it is not a novel discovery. The realistic question for South Africa is whether adjunctive use in depression is covered by the local registration and available in a suitable formulation.

## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the SAHPRA-approved Professional Information (PI) for safety information. Report adverse drug reactions to SAHPRA.

One signal from the retrieved literature: a systematic review of case reports describes impulsive-compulsive behaviours (such as problem gambling, hypersexuality and compulsive behaviours) linked to aripiprazole use or dose increases (PMID 38227009). No drug-interaction records were found for this drug in the source data.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Several completed Phase 3 placebo-controlled trials and multiple meta-analyses support adjunctive aripiprazole in major depression, so the evidence is strong (L1). However, the SAHPRA safety information is missing, the local indication text is empty, and the registered product is an injection, so use should stay within the approved label until these are resolved.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- The SAHPRA package insert (PI) for warnings and contraindications. This is currently a blocking gap.
- Confirmation of the approved indications for the South African registration, and whether adjunctive use in major depression is covered.
- Confirmation of whether oral aripiprazole products are registered in South Africa, since the depression evidence is for oral use.
- Mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank.
- A check of the source record against the regulatory label to decide whether this counts as repurposing at all.

Other predictions for this drug are weaker. Trichotillomania (score 99.26%) is at L3 with two small or upcoming trials, and aripiprazole has also been reported to cause hair-pulling, so its net benefit is unresolved. The remaining predictions (rare genetic syndromes, retinal dystrophy, hydranencephaly and others) are model-only (L5) with no supporting evidence and should be held.
