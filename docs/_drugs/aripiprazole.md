## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. From general pharmacological knowledge, aripiprazole is a partial agonist at dopamine D2/D3 receptors and at serotonin 5-HT1A receptors, and an antagonist at 5-HT2A receptors. This profile allows it to adjust dopamine and serotonin activity when added to an antidepressant.

Major affective disorder covers mood disorders such as major depression. The trials compiled for this report mostly test aripiprazole as an add-on to an antidepressant in patients who responded inadequately, which is a plausible fit with this receptor profile. The mechanistic description comes from general knowledge, not from the supplied data.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions are recorded. Examples include a **Major** interaction with bupropion, and **Moderate** interactions with clarithromycin, cimetidine, cisapride, aprepitant, epinephrine and several antidiabetic agents (metformin, pioglitazone, alogliptin, canagliflozin, acarbose, albiglutide, chlorpropamide, acetohexamide). Anticholinergic agents (atropine, hyoscyamine, clidinium) and loperamide are also listed.
- **Conditions flagged as contraindicated or needing caution (not taken from label text)**: neutropenia, leukopenia and agranulocytosis; hyperglycaemia, diabetes and diabetic ketoacidosis; orthostatic hypotension; epilepsy; myocardial infarction, coronary artery disease and heart conduction disease; neuroleptic malignant syndrome; cerebrovascular disorder; hyperlipidaemia; and obesity.

Warnings and contraindications from the SAHPRA-approved Professional Information (PI) are not available in the data compiled for this report. Please refer to the PI and report adverse drug reactions to SAHPRA.
