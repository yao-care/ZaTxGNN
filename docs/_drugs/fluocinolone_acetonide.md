## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available for this drug. Fluocinolone acetonide is a topical glucocorticoid, and glucocorticoids have well-known anti-inflammatory and immunosuppressive effects. This reasoning comes from the drug class, not from the supplied data.

Lichen planus is a T-cell-mediated inflammatory skin disease, so suppressing the inflammatory infiltrate is a plausible way for a corticosteroid to help. The original indications are also missing from the registration record, so the similarity between old and new indications cannot be assessed.

TxGNN gave the same score (99.42%) to two related variants, hypertrophic lichen planus and lichen planus pigmentosus. The model therefore appears to be predicting a broad lichen planus signal, not a specific one for the annular atrophic form. Pigmentary and atrophic variants may respond differently from classic lichen planus, and this cannot be judged without direct evidence.
