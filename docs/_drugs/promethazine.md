## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the source record. Based on known information, promethazine is an H1 receptor antagonist with anticholinergic activity, and its use as an antihistamine is well established. Mechanistically, it could relieve histamine-driven itch and irritation in the eye.

The link to rosacea conjunctivitis is weak, though. Ocular inflammation in rosacea is not mainly driven by histamine. At most, an antihistamine or anticholinergic could give symptomatic relief. No trials or publications were found, so the prediction should be treated as speculative. A high model score reflects similarity in the knowledge graph, not proof of benefit.
