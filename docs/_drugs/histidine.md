Histidine is an essential amino acid used as a nutritional component in parenteral nutrition and dialysis solutions.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Histidine is an amino acid and the precursor of histamine, and in South Africa it is registered only within multi-component nutrition or infusion products.

The only linked publication examines gastric amyloid pathology and mucosal denervation in hereditary ATTR amyloidosis. It does not test histidine and does not show a histidine-related mechanism. Histamine signalling in gastric motility is a plausible but unsupported connection, so the high model score should be treated as a hypothesis only.
