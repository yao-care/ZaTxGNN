## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available for this drug. Ferrous gluconate supplies elemental iron, which the body uses for haemoglobin synthesis and other iron-dependent processes.

The mechanistic link to this prediction is weak. This form of megaloblastic anemia is, by definition, not caused by vitamin B12 or folate deficiency. Megaloblastic changes usually reflect impaired DNA synthesis rather than a lack of iron. The high score may reflect general proximity between anemia-related conditions in the model rather than a specific biological mechanism. No trials or publications were found to support or refute the link.
