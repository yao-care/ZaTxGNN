## One-Sentence Summary

Ferrous gluconate is an iron salt used as an iron supplement. The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **vitamin B12- and folate-independent constitutional megaloblastic anemia**, with a score of 0.9994. The data compiled for this report contain **no clinical trials and no publications** for this disease, so the prediction rests on the model alone.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available for this drug. Ferrous gluconate supplies elemental iron, which the body uses for haemoglobin synthesis and other iron-dependent processes.

The mechanistic link to this prediction is weak. This form of megaloblastic anemia is, by definition, not caused by vitamin B12 or folate deficiency. Megaloblastic changes usually reflect impaired DNA synthesis rather than a lack of iron. The high score may reflect general proximity between anemia-related conditions in the model rather than a specific biological mechanism. No trials or publications were found to support or refute the link.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

- The prediction has a high model score but no supporting trials or publications (Evidence Level L5). The biological link between iron and a megaloblastic anemia that does not depend on vitamin B12 or folate is unclear.
