## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Celecoxib is known as a selective COX-2 inhibitor that reduces prostaglandin-mediated inflammation and pain.

Acromesomelic dysplasia, Hunter-Thompson type is a rare inherited skeletal dysplasia caused by loss-of-function changes in the CDMP1/GDF5 gene. Its basis is developmental and non-inflammatory. COX-2 inhibition would not be expected to correct this underlying defect, and no credible mechanistic link has been identified.

The high score most likely reflects proximity in the model's network to skeletal and joint conditions rather than a true pharmacological rationale. This prediction should be treated as a computational signal only.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction is supported only by a model score. There are no trials or publications, and the disease is a genetic developmental skeletal disorder with no plausible COX-2-related mechanism. Evidence level is L5.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Any preclinical or clinical study directly addressing this disease, which currently does not exist in the compiled data
