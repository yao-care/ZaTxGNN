## One-Sentence Summary

Fusidic acid is an antibacterial that acts mainly against Gram-positive bacteria such as *Staphylococcus aureus* and is marketed in South Africa as topical products.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **exposure keratitis**, but there are currently **no registered clinical trials** and only **1 indirect publication** (a case series on ocular infections), so the evidence is very limited.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general pharmacology, fusidic acid inhibits bacterial elongation factor G (EF-G), which blocks bacterial protein synthesis. It is active mainly against Gram-positive organisms such as *Staphylococcus aureus*, with limited activity against Gram-negative bacteria.

Exposure keratitis is mainly a mechanical or desiccation disorder of the cornea, caused by incomplete eyelid closure or poor tear film protection. An antibacterial would be relevant only if the damaged cornea develops a secondary bacterial infection. The mechanistic link is therefore indirect.

The only retrieved publication is a case series on ocular infections caused by *Tsukamurella* species. It does not study fusidic acid, and it does not study exposure keratitis itself. The high TxGNN score is a computational result and is not supported by clinical evidence at present.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
- The prediction score is very high, but no clinical trial or publication directly studies fusidic acid in exposure keratitis. The only retrieved paper is an indirect case series, and exposure keratitis is mainly non-infectious, so the mechanistic basis is weak.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Direct clinical or laboratory evidence of fusidic acid in exposure keratitis, or in secondary bacterial keratitis in exposed corneas
- An ophthalmic formulation and route assessment, since current South African registrations are topical skin products only
- SAHPRA Professional Information warnings and contraindications, and mechanism of action data
- A safety review for ocular use, including tolerability and antimicrobial resistance considerations
