## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, ofloxacin is a fluoroquinolone that inhibits bacterial DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV, and its efficacy in bacterial infections is well established.

For polyclonal hyperviscosity syndrome, no plausible mechanistic link could be identified. This condition is a haematological disorder driven by excess immunoglobulin or protein, whereas ofloxacin acts on bacterial DNA replication. The high TxGNN score reflects a graph-based prediction and is not backed by any trial or publication. This prediction should be regarded as a computational artefact until proven otherwise.
