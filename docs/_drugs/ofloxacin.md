## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

It is not, on current evidence. Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Ofloxacin is known to inhibit bacterial DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV, which is how it kills bacteria.

Polyclonal hyperviscosity syndrome involves raised serum viscosity from excess immunoglobulins. Ofloxacin has no known effect on serum viscosity or immunoglobulin levels, so no mechanistic link can be drawn. The very high TxGNN score (99.91%, rank 754) reflects a pattern in the knowledge graph, not any demonstrated therapeutic effect.
