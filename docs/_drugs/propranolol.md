## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Propranolol is a non-selective beta-adrenergic blocker, but the Evidence Pack does not document any pathway linking beta-adrenergic blockade to this rare inherited myopathy.

The high score (99.40%) is a knowledge-graph prediction only. It should not be read as evidence of benefit. Distal myopathy, Tateyama type is a structural muscle disease, and there is no known biological reason for a beta-blocker to alter its course. Until a mechanism is proposed and tested, this remains a hypothesis generated by the model.
