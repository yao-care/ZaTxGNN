## Citalopram: From Depression and Anxiety Disorders to Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Citalopram is a selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI), originally used for depressive and anxiety disorders.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD)**.
The data compiled for this report include **4 clinical trials** (none confirmed to test citalopram directly) and **16 publications**, of which about 8 are citalopram-specific OCD studies, mostly small open-label studies.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. The rationale therefore rests on the known SSRI class mechanism: citalopram selectively blocks serotonin reuptake, and OCD is well recognised as responsive to serotonergic medication (see the review by Zohar et al., PMID 12607204).

The original indications (depression and anxiety disorders) share serotonergic pathophysiology with OCD, which is often treated with SSRIs. Citalopram-specific OCD reports include treatment-resistant adult OCD (PMID 10572334), a concentration-response study (PMID 15089116), a single-blind comparison with fluvoxamine and paroxetine (PMID 9241005), and open-label studies in children and adolescents (PMID 9466233, 12839522).

Other predicted indications (histrionic personality disorder (disease) 0.9968, schizotypal personality disorder 0.9968, paranoid personality disorder 0.9968 and schizoid personality disorder 0.9968) share an identical score and have no or only indirect supporting evidence. They are not recommended for further evaluation at this stage (decision: Hold).

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
OCD is a recognised serotonergic-responsive condition, and there are several citalopram-specific open-label and small comparative studies supporting the prediction. However, no completed controlled trial directly tests citalopram in OCD, so the evidence is mainly class-level and the L2 grading should be treated cautiously.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Review of controlled trials or meta-analyses of citalopram specifically in OCD
- Confirmation of the SSRI used in NCT02022709 and NCT00074815
- SAHPRA Professional Information (warnings, contraindications, approved indications) for the 4 registered products
- Mechanism of action data
- A cardiac safety plan (QT risk, ECG monitoring) and interaction screening, particularly for QT-prolonging drugs and CYP2C19 inhibitors (such as omeprazole and cimetidine)
- A safety review for paediatric use and for patients with bipolar disorder or epilepsy
