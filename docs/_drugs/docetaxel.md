## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, docetaxel is a taxane that stabilises microtubules and causes mitotic arrest in rapidly dividing tumour cells. This antimitotic action is not specific to one tumour type.

Docetaxel's efficacy in several solid tumours, including breast cancer, is already established. Breast carcinoma is listed among its original indications, so the TxGNN prediction essentially recovers a known use. The prediction is mechanistically sound but is not a new repurposing opportunity.

Other predicted indications, such as Ewing sarcoma and rhabdomyosarcoma, are better candidates for repurposing questions. Their evidence is limited to small Phase 2 studies and mostly gemcitabine combinations. For several rare tumours, including primary pulmonary lymphoma, pulmonary blastoma and rare rhabdomyosarcoma subtypes, there is little or no specific evidence. For primary pulmonary lymphoma and small cell lung carcinoma, the retrieved evidence mostly concerns other lung cancers (mainly non-small cell), so the high scores likely reflect knowledge-graph proximity rather than real support.

## Safety Considerations

SAHPRA package insert warnings and contraindications were not available in the data compiled for this report. Please refer to the SAHPRA-approved Professional Information (PI) for these. Report adverse drug reactions to SAHPRA.

- **Knowledge-graph contraindication signals** (not label text): liver disease, gallbladder disease, thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, agranulocytosis, peripheral neuropathy and other peripheral nerve disorders, anaemia, hyperbilirubinaemia (including Gilbert syndrome), diarrhoeal disease, pulmonary oedema, stomatitis, neuritis and cardiac tamponade.
- **Drug interactions** (100 recorded):
  - **Major:** clarithromycin.
  - **Moderate:** aprepitant, metronidazole, cimetidine, eliglustat, miconazole, rolapitant, rosuvastatin, simvastatin, tinidazole and troglitazone.
  - **Minor:** dexamethasone, levofloxacin, prednisolone and prednisone.
  - Several other interactions have an unclassified severity.
  - The strong CYP3A4-inhibitor type interactions (such as clarithromycin and azole antifungals) deserve particular attention.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Docetaxel in breast cancer is supported by multiple large, completed Phase 3 trials, and breast carcinoma is already an established use. The SAHPRA label warnings and indication text were not available, so safety must be confirmed against the PI before any use.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Download and review the SAHPRA package insert for Docetere 80Mg (warnings, contraindications and approved indications). This is a blocking data gap.
- Detailed mechanism of action data (for example from DrugBank).
- A monitoring plan for myelosuppression, hepatic function, neuropathy and fluid retention, plus interaction screening for CYP3A4 inhibitors.
- Confirmation of EML status and local treatment guidelines.
- For the other predicted indications (Ewing sarcoma, rhabdomyosarcoma), treat them as research questions that need dedicated studies. Do not act on the unsupported rare-tumour predictions.
