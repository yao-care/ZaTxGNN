# Dexpanthenol: From Vitamin Supplementation to Anorectal Stricture

## One-Sentence Summary

Dexpanthenol is a precursor of pantothenic acid (vitamin B5). In South Africa it is registered only as a component of the multivitamin infusion Cernevit.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **anorectal stricture**.
**No clinical trials and no publications** currently support this prediction, so it rests on the model score alone.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available. Dexpanthenol is converted in the body to pantothenic acid, a precursor of coenzyme A. Coenzyme A is central to cellular energy metabolism, and pantothenate supports epithelial repair.

The link to anorectal stricture is **speculative**. A stricture is a fibrotic narrowing, and there is no evidence that supporting epithelial repair prevents or reverses fibrosis. The high score most likely reflects the drug's position in the knowledge graph rather than a demonstrated effect.

The only registered product is an infusion. Treating an anorectal condition would probably need a topical or rectal formulation, and route compatibility has not been assessed.
