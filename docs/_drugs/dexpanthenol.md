## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general pharmacology, dexpanthenol is converted to pantothenic acid, a building block of coenzyme A. This pathway is involved in cellular metabolism and in the repair of skin and mucosal epithelium.

A mechanistic link to mucosal healing in anorectal tissue is plausible. However, a stricture is a narrowing caused by fibrosis and scarring, and healing support does not necessarily prevent or reverse it. The link is unverified, and no trial or publication was found to test it.
