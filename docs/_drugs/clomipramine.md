## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Clomipramine is a potent serotonin reuptake inhibitor. Its metabolite, desmethylclomipramine, adds noradrenergic activity. Modulating serotonin and noradrenaline is the established basis for treating anxiety, panic and OCD-spectrum disorders.

OCD is closely related to anxiety disorders. Guideline-level and network meta-analytic literature supports this drug class in anxiety. Clomipramine has also been studied directly in panic disorder and agoraphobia, which are anxiety disorders.

**Caveat:** Most of the listed trials are in OCD, which is the original indication. Direct anxiety-disorder RCT evidence for clomipramine specifically is therefore partly inferred from OCD and panic data. Modern SSRIs are generally preferred first-line because they are better tolerated.

## Safety Considerations

**Contraindications and conditions needing caution** (from the knowledge graph, not from label text): epilepsy, manic bipolar affective disorder, liver disease, gallbladder disease, substance abuse/dependence, malignant hyperthermia, phaeochromocytoma and related adrenal tumours, glaucoma, hyperthyroidism (including Graves disease and thyrotoxicosis), neuroblastoma, and several cancers (duodenum, small intestine, heart, adrenal gland).

**Drug interactions:** The interaction query returned 100 records. Examples of **Major** interactions:
- Epinephrine
- Bupropion
- Lorcaserin
- Potassium citrate
- Cisapride

Examples of **Moderate** interactions:
- Famotidine and cimetidine
- Clarithromycin
- Morphine
- Acetylsalicylic acid
- Anticholinergics (atropine, hyoscyamine, glycopyrronium)
- Loperamide
- Sulfonylureas (chlorpropamide, acetohexamide)
- Bowel-preparation agents
- Activated charcoal

**Other signals from the literature:**
- Rare pancytopenia (case report)
- Sexual dysfunction
- Anticholinergic effects
- Cardiac conduction effects
- Lowered seizure threshold
- Tourettism (case report)

The SAHPRA package insert warnings and contraindications were not available. Please refer to the SAHPRA-approved Professional Information (PI) for full safety information. Report adverse drug reactions to SAHPRA.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Clomipramine has plausible serotonergic and noradrenergic mechanisms for anxiety, and it has older controlled trials in panic disorder and agoraphobia. Guideline-level and network meta-analytic literature supports the drug class. However, the registered trials are mainly in OCD, and better-tolerated SSRIs are preferred first-line.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- The SAHPRA package insert (warnings, contraindications and approved indication), which is currently missing and is a blocking gap for safety screening
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Direct randomised evidence for clomipramine in anxiety disorders
- A monitoring plan covering cardiac (ECG) risk, seizure risk, anticholinergic effects and the high drug-interaction burden
